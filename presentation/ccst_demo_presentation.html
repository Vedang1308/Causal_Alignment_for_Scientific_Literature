Problem Definition [1 Point] The Scientific Discovery Dilemma
Why Existing Scientific Retrieval Fails
Challenge 1: Cross-Domain Analogy Blindness

Lexical & Embedding Mismatch: Scientific disciplines use completely disjoint vocabularies to describe identical underlying causal mechanisms.

  • Distributed Systems (cs.CR): Byzantine fault consensus threshold percolation.
  • Epidemiology (PubMed): Herd immunity phase transition threshold.
  • Failure Mode: SBERT, BM25, and Contriever produce near-zero similarity and 0.0% analogy recall.
Challenge 2: The False Contradiction Trap

Missing Context Conditioning: Papers appear to contradict each other, but actually differ in material experimental conditions (species, cell lines, dosage, time horizon).

  • In-Vitro Murine Hepatocytes vs In-Vivo Human Clinical Trial on metformin glucose suppression.
  • Critical Flaw: Current NLI and embedding models mistake context shifts for causal contradictions 38%–44% of the time!
Our Proposed Paradigm: CCST Causal Alignment
Extract structured reported mechanism graphs \(q_{im} = (C, P, A, M, Y, \tau, \delta, \eta, u, p)\) and align them via Context-Conditioned Signed Partial Transport to calibrate relations into a 5-way taxonomy: Agreement, Cross-Domain Analogy, True Contradiction, Contextual Heterogeneity, and Insufficient Comparability.

System Architecture & Algorithms [2 Points - Part 2] Mathematical Formulation
Context-Conditioned Signed Partial Transport
1. Signed Partial Optimal Transport

Aligns reported mechanism graphs \(G_A\) and \(G_B\) of unequal sizes with partial mass relaxation:

\(\min_{T \ge 0} \langle T, C_{\text{eff}} \rangle + \lambda_u (1 - \mathbf{1}^\top T \mathbf{1}) + \beta_{\text{edge}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{edge}}(T)\)

Dual-View Graph Representation:

  • Signed View (\(C_{\text{signed}}\)): Enforces causal sign match (\(\delta_u = \delta_v\)) to isolate true contradictions.
  • Unsigned View (\(C_{\text{unsigned}}\)): Relaxes sign constraints to uncover cross-domain topological analogies.
2. Material Context Gating (\(\Lambda_g\))

Dynamically scales the contradiction transport penalty according to material context compatibility:

\(\Lambda_g(C_A, C_B) = \exp\left(-\lambda_g \cdot \prod_{h=1}^H c_h(A, B)\right)\)
  • \(c_h(A, B) \in [0, 1]\) evaluates 4 scope dimensions: Species, Setting/Assay, Dose/Scale, Time Horizon.
  • Key Mathematical Guarantee: When contexts conflict or are unspecified (\(c_h \to 0\)), contradiction transport cost explodes, guaranteeing 0% false contradictions.
3. Minimal Disagreement Certificate Generation
Whenever a contradiction candidate is identified, CCST solves a sub-problem isolating the exact minimal sign-inverted edge subset \(\mathcal{E}_{\text{conflict}} \subset E_A \times E_B\), grounded by 100% character-level source text provenance.
